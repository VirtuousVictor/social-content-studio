# Editing and Delivery Planning

Use this stage to shape a cut from supplied or generated material and prepare reviewable delivery instructions. It does not render or export video.

## Define edit intent

State what the first three seconds must accomplish, where product proof lands, where emotion peaks, and how the brand memory point resolves. Keep the story understandable without explanation in a meeting.

## Build the timeline

For each beat or shot, specify order, in/out time, duration, narrative purpose, music beat or cut point, voice-over, subtitles, supers, sound effects, ambience, transitions, and product/logo hold. Match the approved runtime and aspect ratio. Protect readability and safe areas across platform versions.

## Sound and end card

Treat music, ambience, effects, silence, and dialogue as separate decisions. Let the last cut and sound cue resolve the story. When the brief calls for a specific end card, use its approved artwork and account for its timing in the edit.

## Review and delivery versions

Name each version, summarize changes, list export ratios and durations, and note known defects, open approvals, and pending decisions. Keep versions comparable and the change history traceable.
