# Pre-Production Meeting Pack

Use this stage when the user requests a production-ready preparation pack, shoot planning, or a detailed AI/live-action execution brief. Do not add it to an ordinary social content package automatically.

## Confirm scope

Carry forward approved concept, duration, aspect ratio, scenes, talent, product needs, live-action/AI balance, locations, delivery formats, schedule, and budget level. Flag any material missing decision.

## Prepare the pack

Cover:
- Creative recap and film structure.
- Director treatment and visual references.
- Casting, performance, wardrobe, makeup, and hair.
- Art direction, props, locations, set dressing, and continuity items.
- Product preparation, handling, hero views, packaging, and packshot rules.
- Camera, lens, movement, framing, lighting, palette, and texture.
- Music, sound, voice-over, subtitles, supers, and logo/end-card treatment.
- Shot plan, schedule, deliverables, versioning, risks, and approvals.

## Approval and risk control

Separate client approvals, internal choices, and blockers. Name who owns each decision and what cannot proceed without it. Distinguish visual references from locked production choices.

## Handoff

Provide a contents list, section-by-section plan, confirmation items, production risks, materials list, and next-stage owner.
