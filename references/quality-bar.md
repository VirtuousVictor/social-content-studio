# Cross-Stage Quality Bar

For TVC/brand-film requests, also verify the selected narrative model, product-world/brand-world logic, reference-asset dependencies, grid-to-video timing, and whether any model-specific assumption has been checked. Use the advanced film references linked from `workflow-map.md` when those deliverables are in scope.

## Brief and strategy
- Audience, business goal, and communication task are clear.
- Product truth, proof, emotional promise, assumptions, and risks are separated.
- Claims and local safety or legal constraints are supported and respected.
- Creative routes are meaningfully different when options are requested.

## Concept and treatment
- The idea can be stated in one sentence and serves the brief.
- The story outline is not confused with a shot list.
- Visual choices explain why they express the idea.
- References answer a concrete question and are not copied.
- Product and brand artwork remain accurate.

## PPM and storyboard
- Visible elements, approvals, owners, and production risks are accounted for when a PPM is requested.
- Shot timing matches the runtime with no gaps or overlaps.
- Every shot has a narrative or proof purpose and a motivated transition.
- Actor, product, prop, location, light, color, and sound continuity are trackable.

## AI prompts and coordination
- References have clear roles and continuity anchors.
- Camera movement is distinct from subject movement.
- Negative constraints target likely errors without adding noise.
- Versions, pass/fail criteria, and regeneration instructions are traceable when coordination is requested.

## Edit and delivery
- Opening hook, product proof, emotional peak, and brand landing are legible.
- Music, effects, voice-over, overlays, and silence are timed intentionally.
- The ending resolves the story within the requested runtime. Check end-card artwork and duration when specified by the current project.
- Versions, ratios, durations, known issues, and approvals are stated.

## Platform delivery and operation
- Copy is distinct, descriptive, native to each platform, and localized.
- Emojis support readability and match the brand tone.
- No unsupported views, reach, virality, sales, or engagement claim is made.
- File, research, generation, and external-action status is reported accurately.
