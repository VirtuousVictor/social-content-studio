# End-to-End Workflow Map

This is the single-skill routing map. All professional methods are internal references in this skill folder; do not call or require separate specialist skills.

## Classify the work

Read the [active entrypoint](../SKILL.md) for scope, approved assets and deliverable limits. Historical examples apply within those limits.

Identify whether the user needs to create, transform, research, plan, execute, or verify something. Choose the stage from the deliverable, not keywords alone. Confirm what inputs, tools, freshness, risk level, quality bar, and output format are needed.

## Choose the shortest complete route

- One caption or community post: confirmed product profile, platform-copy guidance, quality check.
- One creative idea: brief strategy as needed, then creative direction.
- Proposal: brief, concept, and treatment only if requested.
- Full social video package: brief as needed, creative direction, storyboard, video prompt, platform copy, quality review.
- Full production preparation: approved concept and treatment, PPM, storyboard or production plan as requested.
- AI generation workflow: storyboard, prompts, production coordination, edit/delivery plan.
- Still-image prompt: image-prompt method. If rendering is explicitly requested, use the separate image-generation workflow and only a compatible host tool; this Skill folder does not contain a renderer.
- Review or failed output: revision diagnosis, affected-stage change, continuity check.

## Advanced film route

For TVC/brand-film work, consult `advanced-film-creative-playbook.md` for the entry mode and story model. Add `advanced-visual-assets-and-preproduction.md` when a treatment needs visual proof or when references/continuity assets must be planned. Add `advanced-storyboard-and-film-structures.md` for multi-grid boards, product films, cross-world match cuts, multi-segment videos, or long takes. Add `advanced-image-prompt-library.md` for TVC-specific frame types, style families, and visual diagnostics. These are internal workflows and references; any renderer, external model, or service remains a separately available capability. For Seedance-specific prompting, 15/30-second configuration, scene/pacing, hook, voiceover, casting or generation-result diagnosis, consult the matching source module in `complete-professional-source-library.md`; use only the sections the current task needs.

Do not add PPM, spreadsheets, batch tracking, edit plans, or every possible asset to a routine social package unless requested.

## Readiness gates

Ask before proceeding when unknowns change product truth, evidence, claim legality, safety, audience, target market, channel, language, required duration or aspect ratio, mandatory brand wording, required assets, approval conditions, or the selected execution mode. For other gaps, state a safe assumption. Do not route by keywords alone or ask a long questionnaire when one decision controls the work.

For current claims, market facts, legal or regulatory questions, and niche product details, research authoritative current sources when available. If research or a required tool is unavailable, say what remains unverified and do not fabricate the result.

## Handoff record

For multi-stage work, carry project context, current stage, known facts and sources, assumptions, missing information, approved idea and assets, restrictions, continuity anchors, acceptance criteria, downstream notes, and next stage. Do not proceed past an unresolved gate that affects truth or safety.
