# Brief Strategy and Readiness

Use this module before concept work when the request or product context is incomplete.

## Extract what is known

Record brand and product, category, audience, business goal, communication task, verified claims and proof, competitors if in scope, brand tone, slogans, assets, references, channels, languages, durations, ratios, timing, budget or production mode when relevant, mandatory elements, prohibited elements, and approval constraints.

## Sort missing information

Classify each unknown:
- Must ask: affects product truth, legality, safety, audience, objective, required format, or a decision the user must make.
- Can assume: affects craft but has a safe, reversible default.
- Not needed yet: belongs to a later stage or was not requested.

Do not stop for a complete questionnaire when one or two decisions control the work. For a new product, establish the compact product profile once; then reuse it. For an ongoing product, ask only for current gaps.

For a sparse full-film brief, check channel, audience, product truth, mandatory or forbidden elements, assets, approval constraints, duration and ratio, reference style, intended message, actor availability, and conversion objective. Ask only the items that affect the requested output. If the user authorizes defaults, list them before proceeding.

## Define the communication task

Write one sentence in this form: who should believe or do what, in which situation, and why the product is credible there.

Separate:
- Business goal.
- Communication task.
- Audience tension.
- Product promise.
- Verified proof.
- Desired action.

Do not convert an emotional promise into an unsupported feature or guarantee.

## Prepare creative routes

When the user has not selected an idea and asks for proposal options, develop two to four routes with different audience insights, product roles, story logic, tone, and risks. Avoid cosmetic variations. If the user asks for one script or one prompt, do not force a proposal round.

## Brief handoff

Use references/workflow-map.md to select the next internal stage. Carry confirmed facts, evidence, assumptions, open questions, restrictions, audience, channel, language, format, and acceptance criteria forward.
